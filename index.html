About S & R Crafts

Raw material, made warm

Welcome to S & R Crafts Concrete Creations. We create unique concrete art for your yard and home — from pavers and planters to garden figurines and custom pieces. Each item is handcrafted from cement, cement-all, or plaster of Paris. Choose from our selection of unpainted or beautifully finished painted pieces. Custom orders are welcome. We’re at the Farmer’s Market every Saturday, and we also offer local delivery and meetups.

It’s not your grandma’s ceramics.
