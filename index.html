About S&R Concrete Crafts

Raw material, made warm

Handmade concrete pieces cast in small batches in Milan, New Mexico.

S&R Concrete Crafts creates handmade concrete pieces in Milan, New Mexico. We cast planters, decor, seasonal pieces, and Southwestern-inspired designs one small batch at a time. Choose from painted, finished pieces or raw concrete designs you can leave natural or paint yourself. Raw concrete pieces can be used indoors or outdoors, giving you the freedom to customize them to fit your own style. Custom orders are welcome. We’re at the Farmer’s Market every Saturday, and we offer local delivery and meetups for customers in the local area.

It’s not your grandma’s ceramics.
